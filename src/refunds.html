  • Before work starts: cancel and get a full refund.

What this covers

This policy applies to all payments made to {{legalName}}, trading as {{brandName}}, for our export consultancy services. It forms part of our Terms of Service. Words such as “Engagement” and “Quote” have the meanings given in those Terms. We provide services only and do not sell or ship physical goods, so there are no product returns.

Cancelling before work starts

You can cancel an Engagement at any time before we begin work on it. We will refund everything you have paid for that Engagement in full. The only exception is a third-party cost we have already paid on your behalf with your prior approval (see Third-party costs).

Cancelling after work starts

If you cancel after we have started work, you pay only for the work we have carried out up to the date you cancel. We refund the remainder of what you have paid.

Work carried out is measured by the milestones in your Quote where it has them, and otherwise by the time spent at the rates in the Quote. We will send you a written breakdown with the refund.

Completed work

Once we have delivered the services described in your Quote, the fees for those services are not refundable, except as set out in If we fall short or where the law requires otherwise.

If we fall short

If we have not provided the services as agreed in your Quote, tell us within 30 days of the date the work was due. We will put it right within a reasonable time at no extra cost. If we cannot, we will refund the fees for the part of the services that was not provided as agreed.

If we cancel

If we cancel an Engagement for any reason other than your breach of our Terms, we will refund in full all fees you have paid for work not yet carried out.

Recurring billing

If your Quote provides for recurring billing, such as a monthly retainer, you can cancel at any time by emailing us. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again after that. We do not refund part of a billing period that has already started, except where the law requires it or under If we fall short.

Third-party costs

Sometimes we pay amounts to third parties on your behalf and with your prior approval, such as government or registration fees, testing, certification or translation charges. These are not refundable once paid, because we cannot recover them. If the third party refunds us, we will pass the full amount on to you.

How to cancel or request a refund

Email {{email}} with:

  • your name and the email address you used with us;
  • your invoice or receipt number, if you have it;
  • whether you are cancelling, requesting a refund, or both, and a short reason.

We will acknowledge your request within 2 business days and give you our decision, with a breakdown where relevant, within 10 business days.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Stripe, in the currency you paid in. Refunds normally appear on your statement within 5–10 business days of approval, depending on your bank or card issuer. If your card is in a different currency from the payment, your bank's exchange rate may make the refunded amount differ slightly from the original. We do not control that, and we do not charge any fee for processing a refund.

If you think a charge is wrong, please contact us before raising a dispute with your bank. We can usually resolve it faster. See Payment disputes in our Terms.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy affects rights you have under the consumer law that applies to you. For example, if you are a consumer in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may have a right to cancel a services contract made online within 14 days, without giving a reason. If you ask us to begin work within that period and then cancel, you will pay for the services provided up to the time you told us you were cancelling.
